What to bring when claiming an item

1. Valid Student ID or Faculty/Staff ID card.
2. Item Reference ID (e.g. #L001).
3. Verifiable proof of ownership: ability to unlock device, photo of the item, or description of contents not disclosed publicly.

Lost a Student ID Card?

Immediately report it lost to Campus Card Services to freeze your dining dollars, printing balance, and building access permissions.
